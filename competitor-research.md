# Psychology Today competitor research (Oct 29, 2026)

## Psychology Today (psychologytoday.com)
- Clinician listing: $29.95/month, no contract. Source: https://join.psychologytoday.com/us/signup
- Patients can filter by location, insurance, gender, specialty, remote or in-person, faith, and more. Profiles come with a protected phone number and a free teletherapy platform (ClearHealthCosts summary): https://clearhealthcosts.com/blog/2026/03/more-therapists-express-concern-about-decline-in-psychology-today-referrals/
- GROUP PRACTICES: PT DOES offer a "Group Practice" profile type with an "Our Team" section that lists each clinician (credentials, city, bio, phone, and "Not accepting new clients" tags). Examples:
  - https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/therapists/premier-psychotherapy-pittsburgh-pa/1727086
  - https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/therapists/nova-initia-counseling-haverhill-ma/1807869
  - https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/therapists/haven-psychology-group-wesley-chapel-fl/1752231
  The weakness is that availability is free text written by each clinician, so there's no structured or verified openings data. Nova Initia's page says "immediate openings," yet one of its clinicians is tagged "Not accepting new clients."

## Complaints (quotes)
- Midwest clinician: "My referrals from Psychology Today were anywhere from 5 to 10 a year. For the last two years referrals from Psychology Today are zero... all spam." (ClearHealthCosts, link above)
- r/therapists: "there's been an extreme drop off in communications and referrals" https://www.reddit.com/r/therapists/comments/1vfewdx/
- r/therapists: "you have to update your profile constantly to be in the first page or 2 of results" https://www.reddit.com/r/therapists/comments/1tdaft9/
- Trustpilot: "leaving a vm for 15+ therapists and never hearing back EXCEPT from say, a rep from Grow or TalkSpace" and "only one out of 8 therapist called me back". There are also billing complaints after cancellation. https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.psychologytoday.com
- Prior sources: ChoosingTherapy reviews of PT, TherapyDen, and GoodTherapy (choosingtherapy.com)

## Gaps to exploit
1. Structured, last-confirmed openings per clinician, with stale listings flagged
2. Group-practice-first search: rank practices, use one intake, route to the best-fit clinician
3. Transparent ranking that doesn't depend on editing your bio
4. Response tracking and guaranteed callback; filter out spam and big-box poaching
5. Specialty and city landing pages (EMDR Frisco, play therapy McKinney) for SEO
6. Psychiatry and medication-management filter (psychiatricprovider.com)
7. Simple billing and cancellation
